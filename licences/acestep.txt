The models of Flip, Extend and Cover: ACE-Step 1.5's turbo diffusion
transformer and its VAE, as GGUF files acestep.cpp converted and runs. The
third file of the download, the Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B text encoder, is Qwen's
under the Apache License 2.0; its text is qwen3-embedding.txt.

huggingface.co/ACE-Step/Ace-Step1.5, whose card states "License: [MIT]";
